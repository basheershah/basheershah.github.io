Research

My work spans five connected themes, from signal representations to waveforms, sampling hardware, and learning-based recovery. Labels such as [J15] refer to the Publications page.

Low-PAPR waveforms for 5G/6G

High peak-to-average power ratio (PAPR) limits power-amplifier efficiency in OFDM-based systems. I design Ramanujan-based multicarrier and multiple-access waveforms (RPDM, ORPDM, RPDMA, µ-RPDM, and Modulo-NPDM) with analytical results on PAPR, spectral efficiency, and complexity. I carried out the first software-defined radio implementation and over-the-air validation of RPDM on an NI USRP X310 testbed.

Key papers: [J15], [J13], [J10], [J3], [C15], [S3]

Modulo sampling and high-dynamic-range ADCs

Modulo ADCs fold large signals into a small range, so a low-cost converter can capture high-dynamic-range inputs. I develop recovery algorithms, performance guarantees under quantization, and low-rate hardware prototypes, which led to a US patent application.

Key papers: [J11], [C8], [C10], [D1], [D2], [P1], [S1], [S2]

Model-based deep learning and unfolded networks

Algorithm unrolling turns iterative solvers such as ISTA and ADMM into trainable neural networks. I study when these networks train reliably, with optimization and generalization guarantees, and design faster sparse-recovery algorithms.

Key papers: [J6], [C9]

Ramanujan signal processing and period estimation

I developed signal representations based on Ramanujan sums and complex conjugate pair sums to extract periods and frequencies from short signals. This includes the orthogonal complex conjugate periodic transform, which needs about half the computation of the FFT for complex-valued signals, with applications to ECG and EEG signals.

Key papers: [J1], [J2], [J4], [J5], [J7], [J17], [C13], [C4]–[C7]

Machine learning for antennas and environmental data

With collaborators at Khalifa University and the University of Belgrade, I apply machine learning to antenna modeling and to air-quality forecasting in the UAE.

Key papers: [J8], [J9], [J14], [J16], [C14]
