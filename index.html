About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi, UAE, working with Dr. Nazar T. Ali. I am also a key researcher in the Khalifa University–University of Belgrade Joint Research Collaboration.

From December 2021 to June 2024, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science at the Weizmann Institute of Science, Israel, working with Prof. Yonina C. Eldar, supported by the Faculty Dean Fellowship. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Shiv Nadar University, India, in 2021, advised by Prof. Vijay Kumar Chakka. Before that, I received my M.Tech. in Computational Engineering from RGUKT Nuzvid, where I received the Pratibha Award with a Gold Medal for academic excellence, and my B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from VVIT University.

My research sits at the interface of signal representations, wireless waveform design, modulo-based sampling theory, and model-based machine learning. During my Ph.D., I developed signal representations based on Ramanujan sums and complex conjugate pair sums for extracting periods and frequencies from finite-length signals. One result is the orthogonal complex conjugate periodic transform (OCCPT), an FFT alternative that needs about half the computation for complex-valued signals. At the Weizmann Institute, I worked on recovery algorithms and hardware for modulo-sampling ADCs, and on optimization guarantees for unfolded neural networks. In my current postdoctoral work, I turn these ideas into low-PAPR multicarrier and multiple-access waveforms for 5G/6G, validated on software-defined radio hardware. More about my research →
