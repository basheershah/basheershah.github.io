About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi, UAE, working with Dr. Nazar T. Ali. I am also a key researcher in the Khalifa University–University of Belgrade Joint Research Collaboration.

From December 2021 to June 2024, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science at the Weizmann Institute of Science, Israel, working with Prof. Yonina C. Eldar, supported by the Faculty Dean Fellowship. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Shiv Nadar University, India, in 2021, advised by Prof. Vijay Kumar Chakka. Before that, I received my M.Tech. in Computational Engineering from RGUKT Nuzvid (Gold Medal) and my B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from VVIT (JNTU Kakinada).

My research sits at the interface of wireless waveform design, sampling theory, and model-based machine learning. During my Ph.D., I developed signal representations based on Ramanujan sums and complex conjugate pair sums for extracting periods and frequencies from finite-length signals. One result is the orthogonal complex conjugate periodic transform, an FFT alternative that needs about half the computation for complex-valued signals. Today, I turn these ideas into low-PAPR multicarrier and multiple-access waveforms for 5G/6G, validated on software-defined radio hardware. I also work on recovery algorithms and hardware for modulo-sampling ADCs, and on optimization guarantees for unfolded neural networks.
